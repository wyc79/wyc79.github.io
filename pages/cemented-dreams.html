What做了什么

Cemented Dreams is a third-person action game set inside a parasite-infested building. Players navigate an infected structure where a mechanical Hive grows through the architecture, creating traversal paths and combat spaces.Cemented Dreams 是一款第三人称动作游戏，舞台是一座遭寄生体侵蚀的建筑。玩家穿行于不断异变的内部空间；一座机械 Hive 在建筑中持续生长，逐步形成新的移动路线和战斗区域。

I worked primarily on combat design, gameplay programming, and level design, building core movement and combat mechanics while coordinating closely with artists and producers.我主要负责战斗设计、玩法编程和关卡设计，开发核心移动与战斗机制，并与美术和制作团队密切协作。

Gameplay systems were implemented using a combination of Unreal Engine C++ gameplay code and Blueprint-driven iteration.玩法系统通过 Unreal Engine 的 C++ 玩法代码与 Blueprint 驱动的迭代相结合来实现。

Level Design - Hive Simulation关卡设计：Hive 模拟

I designed a level centered around a parasitic Hive structure growing inside a building, creating traversal paths and combat arenas through environmental transformation.我设计了一关围绕寄生 Hive 在建筑内部生长展开的内容，让环境变化直接生成新的移动路线和战斗区域。

  • The central Hive structure grows into the building while cables from the structure attempt to strangle it.
  • 中央 Hive 不断侵入建筑，建筑缆索则反过来缠绕并压制它。
  • At cable contact points, the Hive generates hexagonal platforms that players can traverse.
  • 在缆索的接触点，Hive 会生成供玩家穿行的六边形平台。
  • Entrances into the Hive are torn open by structural cables, creating layered vertical gameplay spaces.
  • 进入 Hive 的入口被结构缆索撕开，形成层叠的垂直玩法空间。
  • A critical injection point forms around a cable that pierces the Hive wall, granting players the grapple ability.
  • 在一条穿透 Hive 墙体的缆索周围形成了关键的注入点，赋予玩家钩爪能力。

This level structure links narrative worldbuilding with traversal mechanics, turning the parasite's growth into both a visual motif and a gameplay system.这一关卡结构把世界观叙事与移动机制连在一起，使寄生体的生长既成为视觉主题，也成为实际参与关卡变化的玩法系统。
