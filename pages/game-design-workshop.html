Up the River: Variation / Game ModUp the River：变体／游戏 Mod

Why为什么

Build a variation on a shared base game (Up the River) by changing constraints and mechanics, then iterate through structured playtests while keeping a specific experience goal in focus.以共同的基础游戏 Up the River 为起点，改变其中的约束和机制，再通过结构化玩法测试反复迭代，同时始终围绕一个明确的体验目标。

What做了什么

Our variation reworked the original linear track into a circular race-to-treasure loop with environmental tiles (Wave/Reef), Pirate hazards, and a card system that lets players speed up their ship or hinder others. The intended experience was pressure + strategy as players chase points while avoiding danger.我们的版本把原本的线性航道改成环形夺宝赛道，并加入海浪／礁石地块、海盗威胁，以及可用于加速自己或干扰对手的卡牌。目标体验是让压力与策略并存：玩家一边争夺分数，一边设法避开危险。

How (weekly iteration)如何实现（每周迭代）

Week 1 - Core loop and first balance pass (9/21-9/23)第 1 周：核心循环与首轮平衡调整（9/21-9/23）
  • Defined the core loop: travel between Port and Treasure Island on a circular track, using dice + event cards to create risk/reward decisions.
  • 确定核心循环：在环形轨道上往返于 Port 和 Treasure Island，通过骰子与事件卡制造风险和收益之间的抉择。
  • Iterated quickly on pacing and probability: expanded the board, switched from D4 to D6, increased pirate pressure, and tightened card/rule wording so turns stayed snappy (we focused on pacing/balance tuning + clarity).
  • 快速调整节奏与概率：扩大棋盘、把 D4 换成 D6、加强海盗威胁，并精简卡牌与规则文字，让回合推进更利落。我们的重点是平衡、节奏与信息清晰度。
Week 2 - Playtest-driven clarity and V2 polish (9/24-9/30)第 2 周：根据玩法测试提升清晰度，完成 V2（9/24-9/30）
  • Ran a lab playtest and rewrote setup/turn order text for clarity; tuned incentives (e.g., when card draws trigger) to reduce “dead” rolls.
  • 完成一次课堂玩法测试后，重写了开局设置和回合顺序说明，并调整抽牌触发时机等激励机制，减少没有实际影响的掷骰结果。
  • Conducted internal playtests to reduce downtime and edge-case confusion (reshuffle rules, clearer card effects, max hand size), then consolidated everything into a cleaner V2 with a faster, smoother flow (we led setup/turn-order + edge-case clarity fixes and helped consolidate the final V2 rules).
  • 通过内部测试减少等待时间，并理清重洗牌库、卡牌效果和手牌上限等边界情况，最终整合出节奏更快、流程更顺的 V2。我们主导了开局设置、回合顺序和边界规则的文字修订。

This project was selected for in-class presentation.该项目入选了课堂展示。

Mechanics Project: Focused Mechanic ExplorationMechanics 项目：聚焦机制的探索

Why为什么

Practice mechanics-first design: choose a core interaction, prototype fast, and use formal feedback to refine clarity, pacing, and player interpretation.练习机制优先的设计：选定一个核心交互，快速制作原型，并借助正式反馈来打磨清晰度、节奏和玩家的理解。

This project started from randomly drawn prompts. Our team selected four keywords — auction, versus, romance, and solution-finding — and our goal was to turn them into a playable game.这个项目从随机抽取的主题开始。我们团队选定了四个关键词：拍卖、对抗、浪漫与寻找解法，目标是把它们变成一款可玩的游戏。

We specifically wanted to explore how auctions can function as both a competitive system and a narrative device, letting emotional stakes emerge from mechanical choices (instead of scripted story beats).我们尤其想探索拍卖如何既能作为一套竞争系统，又能作为一种叙事手段，让情感张力从机制选择中自然浮现，而不是依赖预设的剧情节点。

What做了什么

Too Bird To Handle is a silent-coordination bidding game about birds courting. Players work together without speaking, reading one another’s intentions and managing risk through a “give or take” action loop.Too Bird To Handle 是一款以鸟类求偶为主题的无声协作竞价游戏。玩家不能交谈，只能从彼此的行动中判断意图，并在“给予或拿取”的循环中共同管理风险。

Built around an auction-style interaction, the experience emphasizes reading intent, managing risk, and negotiating value under social pressure.整个体验围绕拍卖式的交互构建，强调在社交压力下解读意图、管理风险并权衡价值。

How (weekly iteration)如何实现（每周迭代）

Week 1 - From prompt to playable prototype and first external tests (Week 9)第 1 周：从命题到可玩原型，并完成首次外部测试（第 9 周）
  • Used a “yes-and” brainstorm to interpret the prompt, then committed to an auction-inspired, four-player cooperative direction about birds searching for mates.
  • 用“yes-and”式的头脑风暴来解读命题，随后确定了一个受拍卖启发、以鸟类寻偶为主题的四人合作方向。
  • Built a minimum playable prototype: sketched resource and bird cards, established clear win/loss conditions, and iterated internally to smooth the difficulty curve (less swingy, more teamwork).
  • 制作了一个最小可玩原型：勾勒了资源卡与鸟类卡，确立了清晰的胜负条件，并在内部迭代以平滑难度曲线（波动更小、更讲究团队协作）。
  • Ran early external playtests and captured the biggest issues: players misread the game as competitive, terminology (“bid/bird”) was confusing, and setup/visibility needed cleaner structure.
  • 早期外部测试暴露了三个主要问题：玩家误以为彼此在竞争，“bid／bird”等术语容易混淆，开局布置和桌面信息也不够清楚。
  • Turned playtest feedback into concrete fixes (terminology, setup clarity, table layout), and tightened win/loss wording for faster onboarding.
  • 将反馈落实为具体修改：统一术语、理顺开局设置、调整桌面布局，并重写胜负条件，让新玩家更快上手。
Week 2 - Clarity, visual polish, and finalization (Week 10)第 2 周：梳理规则、打磨视觉并定稿（第 10 周）
  • Ran additional external playtests that confirmed stronger engagement and pacing, while still highlighting edge-case questions and moments where the rules needed explicit guidance.
  • 又进行了几次外部玩法测试，确认了更强的投入感与更好的节奏，同时也暴露出一些边缘情况的疑问，以及规则需要明确指引的地方。
  • Validated changes through internal playtests, then did a final consistency/clarity pass across the rulebook, cards, and board to remove edge cases and improve readability (so the experience matched the intended “silent teamwork” tension).
  • 内部测试验证改动后，我们又统一梳理了规则书、卡牌和棋盘，清除遗漏的边界情况并提升可读性，使成品更贴近“无声协作”应有的张力。

This project was selected for in-class presentation.该项目入选了课堂展示。

Final Project: Original Team Game; Role: ProducerFinal Project：原创团队游戏；职责：制作人

Why为什么

The final project was open-ended: define an original experience goal, build a complete prototype, and demonstrate several weeks of playcentric iteration across rules, components, balance, and presentation.期末项目采用开放命题：团队需要确定原创的体验目标，制作完整原型，并在数周内通过规则、组件、平衡和展示的持续调整，体现以玩家体验为核心的设计过程。

What做了什么

Our team built a competitive survival game centered on social hierarchy. Players take on different roles (upper- to lower-class) and must meet role-specific needs inspired by Maslow’s hierarchy while dealing with threats, deception, and negotiation as resources flow from top to bottom each round.我们制作了一款围绕社会阶层展开的竞争型生存游戏。玩家扮演处于不同阶层的角色，需要满足受马斯洛需求层次启发的各自需求；与此同时，资源每轮自上而下流动，威胁、欺骗和谈判也随之发生。

How (weekly iteration)如何实现（每周迭代）

  • Week 1 - Core loop + first playable: We defined the concept, scope, and primary mechanics (roles + descending resources), aligned on the intended player experience, and built an early prototype for quick internal/external playtests. As producer, I helped lock the core loop and tighten the threat and gun rules based on early feedback.
  • 第 1 周：核心循环 + 首个可玩版本： 我们确定了概念、范围与主要机制（角色 + 逐级下降的资源），就预期的玩家体验达成一致， 并搭建了早期原型以进行快速的内部／外部玩法测试。 作为制作人，我协助团队锁定核心循环，并根据早期反馈完善威胁与枪支规则。
  • Week 2 - Clarity + agency pass: We expanded and stabilized systems through playtesting, then improved readability and decision-making: “pet” → love, shifted to a single-winner victory condition, increased lower-class agency (starting hand), and refined the rulebook for fewer edge-case questions. I led the rules and wording cleanup and helped implement the agency and win-condition revisions.
  • 第 2 周：清晰度 + 能动性梳理： 我们通过玩法测试扩展并稳定了各个系统，随后提升了可读性与决策感： “pet” → love，改为单一胜者的胜利条件，增强了下层阶级的能动性（初始手牌）， 并精修规则书以减少边缘情况的疑问。 我主导了规则与措辞的梳理，并协助落实玩家自主性和胜利条件的调整。
  • Week 3 - Balance with simulation support: We ran a focused balance pass supported by 200k-trial simulations, adjusted deck composition, and updated win conditions to reduce “accidental” wins, then validated changes via final playtests. I ran the simulation-backed balance checks and translated the results into concrete deck and win-condition updates.
  • 第 3 周：借助模拟的平衡调整： 我们在 20 万次试验的模拟支持下进行了一轮专注的平衡调整，调整了牌库构成， 并更新了胜利条件以减少“意外”取胜，随后通过最终玩法测试验证了这些改动。 我负责运行基于模拟的平衡检查，并将结果转化为具体的牌库和胜利条件调整。
  • Week 4 - Presentation polish: Finalized the slide deck and recorded the final presentation video. I helped polish the slide deck and prepare the final deliverables.
  • 第 4 周：展示打磨： 完成了幻灯片定稿并录制了最终的展示视频。 我协助打磨幻灯片，并准备最终交付材料。
